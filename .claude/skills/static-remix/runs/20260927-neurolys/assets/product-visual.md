# Neurolys™ — visual description (written after viewing assets/product.png, 490x706)

## Package
A matte DARK NAVY/near-black STAND-UP POUCH (flexible foil pouch with a resealable zipper
strip visible at the top, folded-over top seal) — NOT a jar, NOT a bottle, NOT a blister
pack, NOT a box. Squared-off stand-up pouch proportions, roughly 2:3 aspect.

## Label — printed on the dark navy pouch
1. Logo: a white line-drawn stylised NEURON/BRAIN-CELL icon (branching dendrite shape) inside
   a thin white circle outline, immediately left of the wordmark.
2. "Neurolys" in bold white sans-serif, with a small superscript ™.
3. "GOMMES AU BLEU DE MÉTHYLÈNE" in large bold white caps — the biggest text block on the
   pack, three stacked lines.
4. "CLARTÉ MENTALE & ÉNERGIE CELLULAIRE" — white text inside a thin blue-outlined pill/capsule
   badge shape.
5. "99,9 % PUR" — white text inside a second, smaller thin blue-outlined pill badge, directly
   beneath the first.
6. Three small round white icon badges in a row: crossed-out wheat stalk ("SANS GLUTEN"),
   crossed-out DNA helix ("SANS OGM"), a small leaf ("ULTRA PUR") — each with a one/two-word
   white caption beneath in small caps.
7. "30 Gommes" in bold white caps at the bottom of the pack.
8. Background/ambient scene behind the pouch in generated hero shots (not literally printed on
   the label, but the reference photography style): the pouch sits against a glowing blue
   abstract backdrop (cool blue light, blurred molecule/particle bokeh, a few dark blueberries/
   blackcurrant-like berries scattered near the base, and a warm amber/gold glowing orb in the
   lower right corner) — this is the established hero-shot styling, reusable as a background
   treatment but not part of the physical pack itself.

## Palette
Deep navy/near-black (#0E1526-ish, the pouch body) · white (all pack text/icons) · a range of
COOL BLUES (glow, icon outlines, ambient lighting) · a single warm amber/gold accent point
(background glow only, not on the pack) · small dark blue/black berries as a supporting prop
in hero shots only.

## NEVER
No jar, no bottle, no blister pack, no capsule bottle, no dropper, no tube. It is a DARK NAVY
STAND-UP POUCH with WHITE printed text/icons and a resealable top strip.

## ⚠ Scale
The reference is 490x706 and the label carries several stacked text blocks ("GOMMES AU BLEU DE
MÉTHYLÈNE", two pill badges, three icon badges, "30 Gommes"). Any ad showing the pouch renders
it at **50 %+ of frame height**, largely front-on, or the stacked micro-copy garbles.
