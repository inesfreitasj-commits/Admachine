Ferméa — round 2 topup, 20 ads
================================

Engine: Nano Banana Pro (gemini-3-pro-image-preview), on the client's own Google API key.
Spend: 20 generations + 4 retries (3 transient 503s, 1 reprompt for P4) ≈ 24 calls total.

Why this batch exists
----------------------
A first attempt at this batch ran on Higgsfield (marketing_studio_image) after Pro returned
503 repeatedly. That batch was undeliverable — Higgsfield's output CDN is blocked by this
environment's egress proxy, so nothing could be downloaded, composited, or QC'd — and on
review it also had two real defects: skin texture too mild for the "before" state, and the
cardboard-sign concept left blank instead of carrying real text like the winning ad it
recreates. Pro came back up; this batch is the redo, on Pro, with both fixed at the prompt
level before generation (not patched after).

What changed at the prompt level
---------------------------------
1. Skin texture (BA1-BA6, S1-S3, SU1) — rewrote every "before" description from a light
   "crepey" mention to explicit deep furrows, loose hanging folds, papery translucent
   quality, and a stated requirement that the aging read as obvious at thumbnail size. Also
   pushed the "after" side's improvement further so the gap is unmistakable.
2. SU2 (cardboard sign) — the winning ad this recreates (win_04) has bold handwritten marker
   text on its sign. The first draft asked for a blank cardboard meaning to composite type
   over it later, which would have looked fake next to a photographed handwritten sign.
   Rewrote the prompt to have the model render genuine handwritten marker text directly:
   "J'AI TOUT TESTÉ / AUCUNE N'A FONCTIONNÉ / SAUF CELLE-LÀ" — a paraphrase of win_04's claim
   (checked against winner-copy.md; not verbatim).

Two more defects caught in QC after generation
------------------------------------------------
1. P4 (Trustpilot phone) — first render ignored the "blank screen" framing implied by the
   concept and drew its own stars plus an invalid rating ("5,36" — Trustpilot tops out at
   5.0). The thumb was also pressing directly on that fake content, so a code-only patch
   would have painted over part of the hand. Reprompted for a genuinely blank screen with
   fingers on the phone's bezel only, regenerated, then composited the verified real figure
   (4,8 / 60 452 avis, from product-page.md) cleanly onto the clean screen.
2. SH1 (shelf bestseller) — the model invented a price tag reading "24,90 €", not the
   verified 34,95 €. Redacted back to blank in code, matching the genuinely blank tags
   sitting right beside it in the same shot, rather than print an unverified number.

"USAGE QUOTIDIEN") checked correct and legible on every shot showing the pack. No other

Defects caught and fixed in this batch
-----------------------------------------
- RS2 (hand pulling the last bottle off the shelf) needed 7 generations. The bottle held at
  a sharp diagonal kept garbling its own small-caps subhead line ("RAFFERMIT VISIBLEMENT ET
  NOURRIT LA PEAU" / "USAGE QUOTIDIEN") — a different word broken each time, never all three
  correct together. Diagnosed the tilt itself as the likely cause (foreshortening stressing
  the renderer) rather than treating it as random bad luck; reprompted for the bottle held
  nearly upright instead, and it rendered correctly on the very next try.
- TP2 (ankle macro) rendered a real, recognizable French newspaper masthead ("Le Monde") in
  the background — an unrequested real trademark. Reprompted to explicitly forbid any
  newspaper/magazine/printed publication in the background; regenerated clean.
- TP1 (hip macro) collided with S3 (hand macro) on the duplicate gate at 0.610 (just over
  the 0.60 gate) — both had a similar warm-skin-fill-plus-blurred-greenery composition.
  Moved TP1 indoors (bedroom/wardrobe background) instead of outdoors; dropped to 0.364.
