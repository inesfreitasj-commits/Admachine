# Ferméa™ — visual description (written after viewing assets/product.png, 285x606)

## Container
- Tall cylindrical bottle with softly rounded shoulders, MATTE BLACK opaque plastic.
- BLACK PUMP DISPENSER with a curved swan-neck spout and a ribbed collar.
- Cut out on white in the reference.
- Not a dropper, not glass, not a jar, not a tube.

## Label — printed directly on the black bottle, no separate paper label
Top to bottom:
1. A thin ORNATE GOLD/COPPER BORDER band across the upper body — a repeating
   diamond-and-scroll art-deco motif.
2. A small GOLD BOTANICAL EMBLEM (a stylised flowering sprig inside a rounded shield),
   immediately left of the wordmark.
3. Wordmark "Ferméa" in gold, elegant serif with the acute accent on the e, small ™.
4. "Huile Corps" / "Raffermissante" in WHITE, two lines, clean sans-serif, the largest text.
5. "RAFFERMIT VISIBLEMENT" / "ET NOURRIT LA PEAU" in GOLD/COPPER caps, two small lines.
6. A thin gold rule.
7. "100 ml / 3,4 fl. oz." in white, small.
8. "USAGE QUOTIDIEN" in gold caps, small.
9. A second ORNATE GOLD BORDER band across the lower body, mirroring the top.

## Palette
- Matte black — bottle body, pump
- Antique gold / warm copper (#C98A4B-ish) — borders, emblem, wordmark, accent copy
- White — product name and volume
- Cream / warm beige — the funnel's background colour throughout
- Deep brown-gold (#8A6113-ish) — the funnel's CTA buttons

## NEVER
No amber or clear glass. No dropper or pipette. No jar or tub. No tube. No white or
coloured bottle — it is MATTE BLACK. No silver; the metallic is GOLD/COPPER.

## Note on the reference
285x606 is low. Label micro-copy ("RAFFERMIT VISIBLEMENT…", "100 ml", "USAGE QUOTIDIEN")
will degrade wherever the bottle is small in frame. It survives because the label is
high-contrast white/gold on black with few words. Keep the bottle LARGE in any ad where
the pack must read.
