# VertiZen™ — visual description (written after viewing assets/product.png, 167x517)

## The bottle — a ROLL-ON, not a spray, not a dropper
- A slim CYLINDRICAL CLEAR GLASS bottle filled with a soft SAGE-GREEN liquid, so the glass
  reads green with a paler frosted band at the neck where the liquid ends.
- A BLACK ROLLERBALL CAP: a smooth glossy black dome sitting on a short clear collar. The
  ball is visible as a rounded black hemisphere at the very top.
- Label printed directly on the glass, white text, centred, top to bottom:
  1. A large WHITE "V" mark — a soft rounded chevron, almost a leaf.
  2. "VertiZen" in white with a small superscript TM.
  3. "Soutien Naturel" / "pour les Acouphènes" / "et l'Équilibre" / "Bille (Roll-On)" —
     four lines of small white text.
  4. "10 ml (0,34 fl. oz.)" in small white at the foot.
- Some funnel shots pair it with a plain WHITE CYLINDRICAL OUTER CAP lying beside it.

## Palette
Sage / forest green · white · black (cap) · pale mint (the funnel's section grounds) ·
cream (the winners' anatomy grounds) · red (problem annotations, the circle device)

## NEVER
No pump, no spray, no dropper/pipette, no tube, no jar, no capsules, no tablets. It is a
GLASS ROLL-ON with a BLACK BALL CAP. No gold or silver metallic.

## ⚠ Scale — the reference is only 167x517, the smallest of any product so far
The four-line label ("Soutien Naturel pour les Acouphènes et l'Équilibre / Bille (Roll-On)")
will garble at any small size. So: **any ad showing the pack renders it at 55 % of frame
height or more**, straight on. I checked the funnel tiles for a larger crop — the biggest
bottle anywhere in them is ~138x377, smaller than the supplied packshot. A proper packshot
from the client is free and would be the single highest-value input for future batches.
