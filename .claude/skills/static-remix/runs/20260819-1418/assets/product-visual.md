# ÉveilSens™ — visual description (written after viewing assets/product.png)

## Bottle
- Small squeeze/dropper bottle, ~20 ml, opaque WHITE plastic, softly rounded cylindrical body.
- Long TAPERED CONICAL NOZZLE in translucent frosted white/natural plastic, roughly one third
  of total height, coming to a fine point for drop dosing.
- Ribbed white screw collar between body and nozzle.
- No pump, no glass dropper, no metal. Matte plastic, not glossy glass.

## Label
- Single wrap-around label covering the lower two thirds of the body.
- Background: vivid MAGENTA-to-PINK vertical gradient, hot pink at the edges fading to near-white
  in the centre behind the text.
- Small dark-magenta oval brand lozenge at the top of the label carrying the ÉveilSens mark.
- Centre copy in dark magenta script: "Augmentez votre plaisir. Profitez de l'orgasme."
- Below the copy: a stylised line-drawn petal/flower motif in dark magenta, abstract and botanical,
  with a small droplet descending from it.
- Bottom band: solid magenta strip with small white text "Female Pleasure Enhancer" and the
  Japanese line "女性の快感エンハンサー".

## Retail box (shown in sales-page bundle shots)
- Upright rectangular carton, same hot-pink palette and same petal motif, white text panels,
  barcode lower right. Bundles show the box standing behind the bottle(s).

## Brand palette
- Hot magenta / fuchsia (#D6006E-ish) — primary
- Soft blush pink (#F8D2E4-ish) — backgrounds
- Warm white / cream — bottle body and negative space
- Deep plum-burgundy (#7B1E48-ish) — sales-page CTA buttons and FAQ header bars

## Non-negotiables for generation
The bottle must read as: WHITE opaque body + LONG TAPERED TRANSLUCENT CONICAL NOZZLE +
PINK GRADIENT WRAP LABEL. Never a glass dropper bottle, never a pump, never amber glass,
never a rounded cap.
