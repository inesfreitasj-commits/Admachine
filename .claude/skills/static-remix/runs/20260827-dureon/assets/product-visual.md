# Duréon™ — visual description (written after viewing assets/product.png)

## The bottle — a SPRAY, not a pill bottle, not a dropper
- A short, wide-shouldered CYLINDRICAL WHITE opaque plastic bottle, roughly the proportions
  of a 30 ml travel spray.
- A CLEAR/FROSTED SPRAY PUMP top: a clear ribbed collar and a clear angled nozzle head,
  no cap visible in the reference (cap likely off for the shot).
- Label printed directly on the white bottle, blue and navy on white:
  1. Logo mark: a stylized BLUE MALE SYMBOL (circle with an arrow, ♂) merged with small
     leaf shapes, immediately left of the wordmark.
  2. "Duréon" in dark navy serif, small superscript TM.
  3. Three ingredient lines in navy, smaller italic Latin names beneath each:
     "Extrait de fenugrec" *(Trigonella foenum-graecum)*
     "Extrait de riz" *(Oryza sativa)*
     "L-Arginine"
  4. A thin horizontal rule.
  5. "SPRAY" in large bold navy caps, "COMPLÉMENT ALIMENTAIRE" in small caps beneath it.
  6. A small water-drop icon beside "30 ml / spray".
  7. "Arômes naturels" in small italic at the foot.
  8. A decorative pattern of BLUE GEOMETRIC LEAF/DIAMOND shapes fills the lower-right corner
     of the label, in a mosaic of blue tones (matches the leaf motif in the logo mark).

## Palette
White (bottle) · navy/dark blue (wordmark, ingredient text) · a range of BLUES (logo mark,
decorative leaf mosaic, spray-pump collar) · no gold, no green, no red on the pack itself.

## NEVER
No pill bottle, no blister pack, no capsule, no dropper bottle, no jar, no tube. It is a
WHITE SPRAY BOTTLE with a CLEAR PUMP NOZZLE and a BLUE/NAVY label. No gold or copper trim
(that was a different product this session — Ferméa — do not carry that palette over).
