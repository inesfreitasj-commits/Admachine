## ⚠ RESOLVED CONTRADICTIONS — use the version noted
1. **Active ingredient mechanism.** The sales page's "La science expliquée simplement"
   section attributes the effect to "**un extrait issu d'algues marines récoltées sur les
   côtes françaises**" (a marine algae extract). The product's own LABEL lists no algae at
   all — only fenugrec, riz, and L-arginine. This is a real inconsistency between the sales
   page's descriptive copy and what's actually printed on the pack.
   **USE ONLY THE LABEL'S THREE INGREDIENTS in any ad copy or ingredient callout. Do not
   mention "algues marines" / marine algae — it cannot be verified against the pack and may
   not even be the same product batch's formula.** The "favorise la microcirculation /
   afflux sanguin" mechanism language is fine to use (it's stated generically, not tied to
   the algae claim specifically).
2. **Target age.** The advertorial's intro line says "pour les hommes de plus de **45** ans";
   the sales page FAQ directly answers "Duréon™ a été spécialement conçu pour les hommes de
   plus de **55** ans." The sales page's FAQ is a direct, specific answer to the exact
   question ("is this right for my age?") vs. the advertorial's passing mention.
   **USE 55 ANS** as the stated target age when an ad needs one; safe to depict any adult man
   generally without pinning an exact age otherwise.
3. **Review count.** The winning ad shows "4.8 ★★★★★ (8'452)" as its own badge. The sales
   page's Trustpilot badge says "Note de 4,8 sur Trustpilot" and the footer says "Basé sur
   60 452 avis vérifiés" / "Plus de 60 000 clients vérifiés". These are two different
   figures for two different contexts (that specific ad's own badge vs. the sales page's
   site-wide figure).
   **USE 4,8 / 60 452 avis vérifiés for any NEW ad's social-proof figure** (matches the
   sales page, the authoritative source) — the 8'452 figure belongs only to that one
   winning ad if it's ever reproduced directly, not to new compositions.

## ⚠ Sensitive category — house rules for image generation
This is an erectile-dysfunction / male sexual performance product. Sales page and advertorial
imagery is consistently: adult couples (grey/greying hair, 50s–60s), embracing, in bed or
close, always tasteful/suggestive — bare shoulders, backs, closeness — NEVER explicit,
NEVER nudity beyond what a mainstream ad platform (Meta/Google) already runs for this
category. Match that register exactly: suggestive closeness and emotional relief/confidence,
not graphic content. No genitalia, no explicit sexual acts, ever.
