ArtériVie topup — 20 ads across 3 new devices
===============================================

Engine: Nano Banana Pro (gemini-3-pro-image-preview), on the client's own Google API key.

Why this batch exists
----------------------
Client sent 4 new winning ads plus a fresh product photo, asking for more topups. One of the
4 (the "97 % de plaque en moins" artery-cutaway hero) is already the ad H1 recreated in the
first ArtériVie batch, so it was skipped. The other 3 were genuinely new devices:
- N — a silent MRI/CT/etc. monitor screenshot with a hand-drawn red circle + "?", no
  marketing text at all (a "mystery finding" pattern-interrupt, extending the silent-native
  format already dominant in this client's first winning-ad set).
- P — a dense mechanism/proof-bar hero: headline, a ✓/✗ fact pair, proof badges, a tagline,
  a star-rating + price line.
- F — a fake-newspaper "sponsored content" native: masthead, headline, teaser, a diagram,
  sometimes a pull-quote.

Correction found and fixed before generating
-------------------------------------------------
The prior run's fact sheet listed "60 452 avis" as a verified page number. It is not —
checked every page of the scraped sales page (hero, footer, pricing tiers) and no review
COUNT appears anywhere, only star ratings. The number is identical to a different product's
(ÉveilSens) review count and was almost certainly copy-pasted across products during a
multi-product session. Flagged this to the client, who confirmed it's fine to use going
forward — so it's used throughout this batch, but on client authority, not page-sourced;
documented in assets/product-page.md's correction note.

The duplicate-gate story — three rounds, the real lesson
-------------------------------------------------------------
First pass: 40 of 190 pairs hard-failed the gate (r ≥ 0.60), most severely across the P and F
series — every P concept shared an identical navy/orange colour scheme, a badges row, and a
full-width solid bottom band in the same position; every F concept shared an identical
overhead wood-table newsprint composition with the same badge/headline/teaser/quote
structure. Per the lesson already learned this session on the ÉveilSens CKL batch: rewriting
the words does not move this number — the LARGE SHAPES have to differ.
Round 1 fix: removed the P series' uniform bottom band and badge row (now optional, 3 of 7
skip it), alternated a navy/orange colour-scheme swap across concepts, and regenerated P5
whose base photo (small jar, bottom-centre, huge open top) was a near-duplicate of P3's own
composition. Failures dropped from 40 to 21, with all P-P pairs clean but nearly every F-F
pair still failing — the shared wood-table photography itself was the real driver there, not
just the text.
Round 2 fix: regenerated 4 of 6 F base photos with genuinely different framing — F2 full-bleed
paper with no table visible at all, F3 rotated ~20° with the stethoscope relocated away from
the text zone, F4 paper occupying only half the frame on pale marble (not wood), F6 shot on
desaturated linen fabric instead of wood. Failures dropped from 21 to 12, but F1 (kept
unchanged as the "reference" version) turned out to be the single most-correlated image
against nearly everything, and 4 particular N-series scans (N1, N2, N3, N5) kept correlating
with each other and with the remaining wood-table F's — all shared a similar dark-border/
bright-centre monitor-photo signature.
Round 3 fix: regenerated F1 (pale bleached-oak table, cool daylight, diagram moved to the
page's upper-right instead of bottom-centre) and N1/N2/N5 (tighter crop with minimal monitor
bezel for N1, warmer off-axis lighting for N2, bright normally-lit room instead of a dark
reading room for N5, breaking the shared "mostly-black-background" signature). One quick
compositing-only fix followed: F1's relocated diagram initially overlapped its own headline
text; narrowed the headline's available width to clear it, no regeneration needed.
Result: 0 of 190 pairs over the hard gate, highest pair +0.578 (F3 vs F5, reviewed — both
are recognisably distinct devices, stethoscope-on-wood vs jar-on-newsprint, read as a shared
palette rather than a re-render). Advisory list (winners, gate 0.45, not a hard rule): nothing
crosses 0.46; highest is N2 at 0.452 against win_05 (an icon-only ad with almost no shared
structure — reviewed, not a re-render).
