Shown as BOX + TUBE together, box left, tube right, slightly overlapping.

## The BOX — white card, portrait, tall
- A GREEN SWOOSH sweeps across the top and a green band runs down the left edge and base.
- Top right: a GREEN OVAL BADGE with white text "100 % NATUREL" and a tiny leaf mark.
- "DÉTOX" / "LYMPHATIQUE" in large DARK-GREEN BOLD ITALIC CAPS, two lines.
- "VeinoVie™" in dark green inside a WHITE ROUNDED BOX with a thin green outline.
- A PHOTO PANEL: a pair of pale human legs from the knee down, bare feet, visibly swollen.
- Overlapping the legs at lower right, a RED-OUTLINED CIRCLE containing GOLDEN-AMBER
  translucent beads in a small dish.
- Three dark-green bullets: "• Améliore le drainage lymphatique" / "• Réduit la sensation de
  lourdeur" / "• Améliore la circulation sanguine".
- Foot: "POIDS NET : 20 g / 0,71 oz" in small dark green.

## The TUBE — GREEN plastic squeeze tube, white flip cap at the top, flat crimped base
- Same "100 % NATUREL" green-and-white badge.
- "DÉTOX LYMPHATIQUE" in WHITE bold italic caps.
- "VeinoVie™" in green inside the same WHITE ROUNDED BOX.
- The same swollen-legs photo panel and the same red circle of amber beads.
- The same three bullets, in WHITE.
- "POIDS NET : 20 g / 0,71 oz" in white at the foot.

## Palette
Dark forest green (#1B7A3E-ish) · bright leaf green · white · pale mint green (the funnel's
section grounds) · golden amber (the beads) · red (the circle outline) · skin tones.

## NEVER
No jar, no pump bottle, no dropper, no pill bottle, no sachet, no blue packaging. It is a
GREEN TUBE and a WHITE BOX. No gold or silver metallic.

## Scale — the rule that matters this run
The client had to hand-fix garbled box lettering on the last product. So on this run:
**any generated pack is at least 50 % of the frame height**, and for flat-ground studio ads
the real packshot is COMPOSITED IN CODE instead of generated. The tube's own micro-copy
(three bullets, POIDS NET) will not survive generation at small sizes.
