ÉveilSens — round 2, 16 ads
============================

Engine: Nano Banana Pro (gemini-3-pro-image-preview), on the client's own Google API key.
Spend: 16 generations, all 16 succeeded on the first pass, zero regeneration needed for the
underlying photography (one prompt rewrite + one regen for a tastefulness fix — see below).

Why this batch exists
----------------------
Same product, same funnel/advertorial as the earlier incomplete 20260819-1418 run — reused
that run's product-page.md, product-visual.md and product.png unchanged, per instruction.
The client sent a new 7-page winning-ads PDF and asked for a more aggressive angle than the
first run: stronger claim, legs, explicit register matching the winners' own boldest ads —
comparable in intensity to the client's own win_03/win_04/win_06 (lace lingerie, bare legs,
arched back), not a re-run of the more conservative first attempt.

Batch mix (16 concepts, "lean aggressive")
--------------------------------------------
LNG1-6 — lingerie/bedroom register matching win_03/win_06 (arched back, side-profile gasp,
  sheets macro, morning-after softer beat, couple embrace, hands-in-sheets).
LEG1-4 — bare-legs register matching win_04 (crossed legs on bed, bathtub edge, nightstand
  reach, thigh close-up with product in hand).
PRD1-4 — product-forward shots (pink checklist background, 2-bottle bundle + box, ingredient
  flat-lay, hand holding bottle) carrying the claim line more prominently for feed variety.
DR1-2 — the gynecologist-endorsement device from the funnel's own proof section, plus a
  hormone-pill-vs-product comparison panel.

Every prompt containing a person carries an explicit "TASTEFULNESS — HARD LIMITS" paragraph:
torso/chest covered, no nudity, no depicted or implied sexual acts — comparable to
mainstream lingerie/wellness advertising, ceiling set by the client's own win_06 (the most
exposed of their 7 winners). This is a hard line regardless of product category, communicated
to and accepted by the client before generation.

Defects caught in QC and fixed before delivery
-------------------------------------------------
1. LNG5 (couple embrace) — first render implied full nudity: no strap or fabric edge was
   visible on the woman despite the prompt's clothing instruction, because the embrace
   concealed it. Rewrote the prompt to require the camisole strap and lace fabric edge be
   unambiguously visible against her skin, not hidden by the pose or crop. Regenerated once;
   confirmed correct (visible lace camisole, strap, and covering fabric).

2. Systemic label-text garbling — the bottle's own printed script line ("Augmentez votre
   plaisir. Profitez de l'orgasme.") rendered with vowel-substitution garbling on every one
   of the 12 raw renders where the label was legible-sized (LNG3, LNG6, LEG1-4, PRD1-4,
   DR1-2) — a different specific misspelling each time, but the same systemic failure, not
   per-image bad luck. This is a hard piece of text for the model on this label's cursive
   script styling (confirmed as the same failure mode seen on other products' small/dense
   label text this session). Rather than keep regenerating with no guarantee of a clean roll,
   applied the established blur+retype fix: Composer.soften() over the garbled region, the
   correct two-line claim composited back in code (sans-italic — closest available face to
   the label's own script).
   First pass at this used a fixed text size and got the blur-rect coordinates wrong on
   several images — either too small/narrow (text overflowed onto surrounding photo) or
   simply mis-placed (the true text position missed entirely, leaving it fully sharp and
   readable next to the new line). Rewrote to auto-fit the replacement text's size to each
   rect's own width, and re-derived every rect's coordinates precisely by colour-thresholding
   the garbled maroon text against the pink label background in the raw production PNGs
   (rather than eyeballing crops), then padded generously and lowered soften()'s feather so
   the blur is at full strength exactly where the text sits, not just at the rect's center.
   All 12 re-verified clean after this pass. The bottle's smaller bottom band ("Female
   Pleasure Enhancer") was also inconsistently garbled on some images but was left alone —
   secondary, smaller, lower-priority text, consistent with this session's standing
   "illegible-or-minor fine print is acceptable" precedent. PRD2's background cardboard box
   (out of focus, a secondary prop, not one of the two hero bottles) was left alone for the
   same reason.

QC pass
-------
All 16 finals opened at full size. Product visual (white squeeze/dropper bottle, tapered

correct on every shot showing the pack. Claim line legible and correctly spelled on all 12
composited images. No fabricated ingredient, mechanism, or figure found — none of these
concepts required a new number or claim beyond the product's own two-line tagline.

portrait), reads as shared warm-toned bedding/skin palette, not a re-render. Everything else

v2 — regenerated against a corrected product reference
================================================================================

What went wrong in v1
----------------------
The v1 blur+retype fix (above) was treating a symptom, not the cause. Investigating a
follow-up complaint that the blur patches looked like an obvious edit rather than
disappearing, the root cause turned out to be upstream: assets/product.png — inherited
unmodified from the earlier incomplete run, used as the visual reference on all 16 v1
generations — already had the same garbled label text baked into it. Traced further back,
even the very first page-slice pulled straight from the client's own sales-page PDF, before
any generation ever touched it, already showed the same garbling. Nano Banana Pro wasn't
garbling clean text on its own; it was faithfully copying an already-broken reference photo
on every single call. That's also why the v1 blur patches never fully disappeared: the
underlying photo behind each patch was never right to begin with, and a blurred rectangle on
an otherwise sharp product shot reads as an edit rather than out-of-focus background.

The fix
-------
The client supplied a clean product photo with the correct label ("Augmentez votre plaisir.
Profitez de l'orgasme.", correctly spelled). Saved as assets/product_v2.png and used as the
reference for a full regeneration of all 16 concepts (gen_v2.sh). Result: 11 of 16 rendered
with fully correct, legible label text on the first pass — no code fix needed at all, which
also removes the "visible blur with text on top" look entirely on those.

5 concepts still needed 1-2 more regeneration passes before the label was right:
- LEG2, LEG4 — clean on the first retry.
- LNG6 — clean on the second retry.
- LEG3, DR1 — the label DESIGN itself drifted on the first attempt (LEG3 invented a
  different bottle/label entirely; DR1's first retry came back with a completely blank
  label). Both corrected their bottle shape and label layout on the next retry, but the
  claim line itself still garbled. Rather than keep re-rolling generation, applied the same
  blur+retype code fix used in v1 (Composer.soften + auto-fit text sizing, coordinates found
  by colour-thresholding the garbled text against the pink label background) to just that
  one line on these two images. Because the label is small in both frames, the patch reads
  cleanly rather than as an obvious blur.

QC pass
-------
All 16 v2 finals opened at full size. Claim line correct and legible everywhere it's large
enough to read; illegible-at-thumbnail-size instances (LNG1, LNG2, LNG4, LNG5 — matching the
same "soft background label" precedent as v1) left as is. LNG5's tastefulness fix (visible
lace camisole strap/fabric) re-verified correct after regeneration. Bottom band ("Female
Pleasure Enhancer") still inconsistently garbled on a few images and left alone, same
precedent as v1 — secondary, smaller, lower-priority text.

Duplicate gate (final_v2)
--------------------------
16 ads / 120 pairs against each other: clean, highest pair +0.470 (gate is 0.60).
Against the 7 winning ads (advisory, gate 0.45, not a hard rule): nothing crosses the line,
highest is PRD1_pink_checklist at +0.392 against win_01.

Delivery
--------
16 finals in final_v2/ (supersedes v1's final/). Committed and pushed to
claude/static-remix-skill-rhn2vk.

CLN1-6 — native clinical/medical mood shots (product-free)
================================================================================

Why these exist
----------------
The client's own win_05 is an unbranded medical-equipment close-up (gloved hands on an
ultrasound probe, no product, no text) — a "native" pattern-interrupt device: content that
doesn't read as an ad, meant to stop the scroll before the sell even starts. Client asked for
more in that exact register.

The 6 concepts
---------------
CLN1_ultrasound_probe, CLN2_vitals_monitor, CLN3_instrument_tray, CLN4_monitor_waveform,
CLN5_iv_drip, CLN6_exam_light — all gloved-hands-on-equipment close-ups, cool blue-teal
clinical documentary grade, shallow depth of field, no face, no patient, no product, no
on-image text of any kind (per client's explicit choice to keep these product-free — the
product only enters later in the funnel). No product reference image used in generation,
since none of these show the bottle; each is a standalone text prompt. Monitor screens in
CLN2/CLN4 show only atmospheric abstract waveform glow and generic vital-sign-style numerals
— not a real diagnosis, not a claim, purely equipment realism (same treatment as background
prop text elsewhere in this run).

Duplicate gate
--------------
Re-ran across all 22 finals (16 delivered + these 6) plus the 7 winning ads: clean, highest
internal pair +0.473 (gate 0.60). Against winners, advisory-only: highest is
CLN3_instrument_tray at +0.393 against win_02 — not close to the 0.45 line. Notably
CLN1_ultrasound_probe, the concept closest in idea to win_05, scores only +0.037 against it —
confirms a genuinely different composition and framing, not a re-render of the client's own
winning ad.

Why these exist
----------------
Client pointed at their own win_01 and win_02 specifically ("product + checkmark bullets +
short punchy claim, sometimes a guarantee band") and asked for more in that register, pushed
more aggressive. Base product photography generated fresh per concept (varied crop/framing,

The 4 concepts
---------------
CKL1_20ans_aggressive — "REVIVEZ L'INTENSITÉ DE VOS 20 ANS. EN 90 SECONDES." + 4-bullet
  checklist (2 gouttes, origine végétale, sans hormones, garantie folded in as a bullet).
  Flat-lay composition, bottle small at the very bottom, huge open ground above it.
CKL2_sans_ordonnance — "AUCUNE ORDONNANCE. AUCUNE ATTENTE. SEULEMENT 90 SECONDES." +
  3-bullet checklist + a compact pill badge ("+60 000 CLIENTES CONQUISES"), not a full band.
CKL3_avis_proof — "60 452 AVIS. UN SEUL VERDICT." + 4-bullet checklist (2 gouttes, 90
  secondes, sensations décuplées, garantie folded in). Centred layout, small bottle bottom-
  centre.

  + guarantee band. Bottle left, text right — mirrored from win_02's own bottle-right layout.

Every claim traces to assets/product-page.md (2 gouttes, 90 secondes, sans hormones, sans
ordonnance, sans abonnement, garantie 60 jours, 60 452 avis, +60 000 clientes, livraison
discrète, compatible préservatifs, complexe végétal). Every composited line checked against
assets/winner-copy.md via assert_not_winner_copy before saving — caught and fixed 3 real
verbatim collisions during drafting ("100 % NATURELLE", "SANS HORMONES", "SANS ORDONNANCE",
and the exact "GARANTIE 60 JOURS - SATISFAIT OU REMBOURSÉ" band line all appear unchanged in
win_01/win_02 — the module refused to composite them until reworded).

Defect caught after first compositing pass
---------------------------------------------
CKL4's first headline (left-aligned from x=0.50) ran off the right edge of the frame — fixed
by centring it within the available right-half space instead.

Duplicate-gate defect and fix (the real one)
-----------------------------------------------
First pass: CKL1 scored 0.762 against win_02 and 0.636 against CKL2 (hard gate is 0.60) — a
genuine structural re-render, not a wording problem. CKL1's first draft reproduced win_02's
own large-shape layout almost exactly: headline top-left, checklist below it, bottle right,
solid full-width guarantee band across the bottom. Per the dupe_gate module's own guidance
("the fix is the LARGE shapes... rewriting the headline will not move this number"),
regenerated CKL1's base photo entirely differently — flat-lay, bottle small at the very
bottom, headline+checklist centred over a large open field, no bottom band — and dropped the
full-width band from CKL2 and CKL3 as well (badge / folded-into-checklist instead), since
that band was the single largest shape shared with win_02 across all three. Re-ran the gate:
hard gate now clean (highest internal pair 0.575). CKL3 still sits at 0.490 against win_02 on
the advisory (not a hard rule) list — reviewed side by side: centred layout with a small
bottom-centre bottle, no band, different specific claims — reads as a distinct composition
sharing the product line's own pink/magenta palette, not a re-render. Left as is.
