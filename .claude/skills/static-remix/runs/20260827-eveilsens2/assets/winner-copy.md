## Note on register
win_03, win_04 and win_06 are the aggressive end of this set — lingerie, legs, a partially
exposed back/hip. That is the ceiling for new concepts: suggestive, tasteful lingerie/skin
exposure comparable to mainstream intimates advertising — never full nudity, genitalia, or
depicted sexual acts.
