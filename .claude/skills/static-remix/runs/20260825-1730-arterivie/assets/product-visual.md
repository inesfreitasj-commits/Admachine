# ArtériVie™ — visual description (written after viewing assets/product.png, 370x679)

## Container
- A SHORT WIDE CYLINDRICAL SUPPLEMENT JAR in opaque WHITE plastic, with a matching WHITE
  SCREW-ON LID with a lightly ribbed edge. Squat proportions — roughly as wide as it is tall
  below the cap. NOT a bottle, NOT glass, NOT a roll-on, NOT a tube, NOT a pouch.
- Contents are WHITE CAPSULES (visible loose beside the jar in winning ad 1).

## Label — printed on the white body
1. Logo: a solid ORANGE-RED HEART, tilted, with a small cutaway artery-and-plaque motif
   inside it in white/dark, sitting immediately left of the wordmark.
2. "ArtériVie" in dark charcoal, clean geometric sans, with a small superscript ™.
3. "Soutient naturellement" / "votre système circulatoire" — two lines, mid-grey, small.
4. "COMPLÉMENT ALIMENTAIRE" in orange caps inside a thin ORANGE OUTLINED PILL box.
5. "RENFORCE" in dark charcoal caps, then "LE CŒUR ♥" larger with a small orange heart,
   then "ET LES ARTÈRES" smaller — three stacked lines.
6. A large ORANGE COLOUR FIELD sweeps across the LOWER THIRD of the jar and curves upward
   along the RIGHT side, with a soft curved boundary — not a straight band.

## Palette
Orange (#F1601E-ish, the funnel's CTA colour) · white · dark charcoal · mid-grey ·
pale peach (#FDEEE8-ish, the funnel's section grounds) · red (the winners' headline accent)

## NEVER
No amber or clear glass. No dropper. No roll-on. No tube. No sachet. No blister pack.
It is a WHITE PLASTIC CAPSULE JAR with a WHITE LID and an ORANGE lower field.

jar renders it at **55 % of frame height or more**, straight on, or the micro-copy garbles.
