   la deuxième et la troisième semaine", and all three testimonials say 3, 4 and 6 weeks.
   The CLIENT'S BRIEF says 7 jours, so 7 jours is what goes on the ads — but never pair
   "7 jours" with a testimonial that says three weeks in the same frame.

   the FIFTH product running. Never put these in a French ad.
