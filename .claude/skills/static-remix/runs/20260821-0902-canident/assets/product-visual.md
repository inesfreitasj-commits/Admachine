# Canident™ — visual description (written after viewing assets/product.png, 437x661)

The pack is ALWAYS shown as BOX + BOTTLE TOGETHER, box on the left, bottle on the right,
slightly overlapping. Both winning ads do this. Never one without the other.

## The BOX — white, portrait, roughly 2:3, matte card
Top to bottom:
1. A ROYAL-BLUE CIRCULAR LOGO — a stylised white dog's head in profile with a small tooth
   and star mark inside — immediately left of the wordmark.
2. Wordmark "Canident" in bold navy sans-serif with a small superscript ™.
3. "NETTOYANT DENTAIRE" / "ANTI-TARTRE POUR" / "CHIENS" — NAVY BOLD CAPS on three lines,
   over a pale blue water-splash graphic.
4. A ROYAL-BLUE BAND with white caps on two lines: "ÉLIMINE LE TARTRE" /
   "ET RAFRAÎCHIT L'HALEINE", with a small white foam-dollop icon at its right end.
5. A PHOTO PANEL: a BLACK-AND-WHITE BORDER COLLIE in three-quarter view, mouth slightly
   open, with a human hand holding the white bottle to its muzzle and white foam visible.
6. Below the photo, WHITE FOAM with round bubbles over blue.
7. TWO WHITE ROUNDED PANELS side by side, labelled "AVANT" and "APRÈS" in small navy caps:
   AVANT shows a tooth with BROWN-YELLOW TARTAR down its root and RED inflamed gum;
   APRÈS shows the SAME tooth clean and white with healthy PINK-RED gum.
8. Foot: "Contenu net : 60 ml / 2 fl. oz" in small navy.

## The BOTTLE — white opaque cylinder, FOAMER PUMP
- A WHITE FOAMER PUMP HEAD with a short, straight, slightly angled nozzle — not a trigger
  spray, not a mist atomiser, not a dropper.
- Same blue circular dog logo + navy "Canident™" wordmark.
- "NETTOYANT DENTAIRE ANTI-TARTRE POUR CHIENS" in navy caps on four lines.
- The same royal-blue band "ÉLIMINE LE TARTRE ET RAFRAÎCHIT L'HALEINE" with the foam icon.
- The same black-and-white border collie photo panel.
- "60 ml / 2 fl. oz" in navy at the foot.

## Palette
- White — box, bottle, foam, and the ads' ground
- Royal / navy blue (#1B4F9C-ish) — logo, wordmark, headline type, bands, CTA bar
- Pale blue — water-splash graphic and bubbles
- Teal-green — the "Canident™" signature in winning ad 1 only
- Brown / tan (#A97A3C-ish) — tartar
- Salmon-pink to red — gums
- Black and white — the border collie

## NEVER
No amber or coloured bottle — it is WHITE. No trigger spray, no mist atomiser, no dropper,
no pipette, no tube, no sachet, no tub. No gold or silver metallic. No dark background on
the pack. And NEVER the competitor pump labelled "PET ORAL TARTAR REMOVER" that appears in
three of the client's own funnel photos.

## Scale — the exception to the standing rule
In this system the PACK IS THE HERO, not a prop. In the winners it occupies roughly the
right-hand HALF of the frame and about 70 % of the frame height. The usual
"middle distance, never dominant" rule is for scenes containing PEOPLE; it does not apply
to these flat-ground pack ads. In any concept that does contain a person or a dog, the
usual rule applies again.

## Note on the reference
437x661 is workable — better than the last product's 285x606. The label survives because
it is high-contrast navy on white with few words. Keep the pack large wherever the box
copy must read; at small sizes the four-line bottle text will degrade first.
