# Lumélia™ — visual description (written after viewing assets/product.png, 551x633, cutout on transparent)

## Container
- A short, wide GREEN opaque glass/plastic jar with rounded shoulders, roughly the proportions of a 50 g cosmetic cream jar.
- A WHITE PLASTIC PUMP DISPENSER top: a tall pump neck with an angled nozzle, all white.
- Cut out on transparent in the reference.
- Not a tube, not a dropper bottle, not a clear jar — the body is opaque solid green.

## Label — printed directly on the green jar, white/dark-green text and small icons
Top to bottom:
1. A small white leaf/botanical emblem to the left of the wordmark.
2. Wordmark "Lumélia" in white script/serif with small TM, on a dark green band near the top.
3. "CRÈME NATURELLE" / "ANTI-TACHES ET MARQUES" in white bold caps, two lines (the largest, most prominent text).
4. A dark green horizontal band: "SOIN CONTRE LES TACHES DE NAISSANCE" / "ET LES TACHES DE PIGMENTATION" in white caps, two lines.
5. "Formule à triple action" in white bold.
6. Three small white icons (crocodile, algae/leaf, chamomile flower) side by side.
7. Three bullet lines in white: "Huile de crocodile : Régénère la peau en profondeur." / "Extrait d'algue brune : Aide à unifier le teint." / "Extrait de camomille : Apaise et prend soin de la peau."
8. "POIDS NET 50 g (1.7 oz.)" in white, small, near the bottom.

## Palette
- Solid GREEN (mid-to-dark green, matte) — jar body
- White — pump dispenser, most label text and icons
- Darker green band — behind the wordmark and the "soin contre..." lines

## NEVER
No clear glass, no gold accents, no blue label, no tube. It is a SOLID GREEN JAR with a WHITE PUMP top and WHITE label text/icons. Do not carry over any other Olethra product's palette (gold Ferméa, navy Duréon, orange ArtériVie, gold-oil-and-glass Fermélia).
