FIXED IN THIS BATCH
  The first M2 had TWO PEOPLE in it — the client spotted it. Cause: the brief said
  "both arms raised out to the sides", which only works from a FRONTAL view. The model
  rendered her side-on, so both arms ran off to the same edge and the upper one read
  as a second person standing behind her. Regenerated with "ONLY ONE ARM APPEARS
  ANYWHERE IN THE FRAME" plus an explicit one-person clause. $0.25.
  RULE FOR NEXT TIME: never write "both arms" / "both legs" unless the camera position
  is also pinned to frontal. Name the number of limbs that may appear in the frame.

COPY — DELIBERATELY RESTRAINED, AND WHY
  All four carry "Peau des bras très relâchée ?" / "16 huiles testées : le classement".
  The image supplies the aggression; the copy claims nothing.
  The harder line — "Avant d'en arriver là…" — implies the oil prevents surgery. That
  is a medical claim the funnel does not make anywhere, and it is the kind of implied
  claim that gets an account restricted rather than just an ad rejected. It is a free
  re-composite if the client wants it, but it is their decision, not mine to assume.
