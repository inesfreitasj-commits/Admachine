# The winners' own on-image copy — never reuse a line from here

Read this before writing a single word that will be composited onto a VertiZen ad.

## Why this file exists

The pixel duplicate gate scores a re-render of a winner correctly when the PICTURE matches —
it flagged three at r = 0.61–0.71. It scored two others at 0.359 and 0.370, comfortably
clean, and the client rejected both on sight as "completely the same". It was right and they
were right: those two didn't copy the picture, they copied **the words**.

- `A6` reused win_05's headline (both lines) and all three of its ticks, changing one word:
  *Absorption* → *Soulagement*.
- `A7` reused win_04's lockup, its badge, and **its handwritten line verbatim**, changing
  only 13 ANS → 25 ANS.

A pixel signature cannot see that. So the copy has to be checked as copy.

**Rule: a composited line that appears verbatim in a winner is a copy, not a variation.**
Same claim, different words is fine — that is what an iteration is. Same words is not.

## How to use a winner's line without copying it

The claim is theirs to keep — it is proven. The wording is what has to move.

| Their line | A variation, not a copy |
|---|---|
| Fini les vertiges. / Fini les acouphènes. | La pièce ne tourne plus. |
| 13 ANS DE VERTIGES AU QUOTIDIEN… | « Je n'osais plus conduire. » |
| SOULAGÉ EN 5 MINUTES ! | 5 MINUTES. C'EST TOUT. |
| Un seul geste, et tout arrête de tourner. | Deux passages derrière l'oreille. |
| NERF SURSOLLICITÉ | OÙ ÇA SE PASSE VRAIMENT |
| Agissez sur la cause, pas seulement sur les symptômes. | Le vertige se voit ici, pas dans la pièce. |

Everything in the right-hand column is still page-supported. None of it is theirs.
