# Concept 05 — US VS THEM (fresh angle, proven DR format)

## Scene
A hard vertical split-screen advertisement. LEFT HALF: cold desaturated grey-blue, a cluttered spill of white hormone tablets and blister packs on a clinical surface, unappealing. RIGHT HALF: warm blush-pink and cream, bright and calm, the ÉveilSens bottle standing alone and elegant with a few cherry-blossom petals. Strong visual contrast between the two halves.

## Text overlays
Bold text at the top of each half.
Left half, white text: LES HORMONES
Right half, dark-magenta text: ÉVEILSENS
Small white text at the bottom of the left half: Ordonnance · effets secondaires
Small dark-magenta text at the bottom of the right half: 100 % naturelle · sans hormones

## Headline
Sans ordonnance. Sans hormones. Sans compromis.

## Caption
Une formule 100 % naturelle et végane, sans hormones ni parabènes. 1 à 2 gouttes suffisent.

## Variation axis: what sits on the THEM side
- var_01: Hormone tablets and blister packs on the left.
- var_02: A generic unbranded white lubricant tube, squeezed and messy, on the left instead of tablets.
