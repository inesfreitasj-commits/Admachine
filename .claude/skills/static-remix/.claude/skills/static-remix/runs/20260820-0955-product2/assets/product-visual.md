# ArtériVie™ — visual description (written after viewing assets/product.png)

REFERENCE: assets/product.png — 1440x2610, cropped from the client's own winning ad
(img_0005) because the supplied packshot was only 367x686. Every label line is crisp
in this version, which is what keeps generated labels legible.

## Container
- Squat CYLINDRICAL supplement pill jar, wider than it is tall-ish, opaque GLOSSY WHITE plastic.
- Wide RIBBED WHITE SCREW CAP, flat-topped, roughly a fifth of total height, with two
  visible moulding rings around it.
- Not a dropper. Not glass. Not a pouch. No pump, no nozzle, no pipette.

## Label — a single wrap, white upper / orange lower, split by a sweeping curve
Top to bottom:
1. Logo mark: an ORANGE HEART in outline, with a cut-away artery cross-section inside it
   showing pale dots, sitting left of the wordmark.
2. Wordmark "ArtériVie" — orange, mixed case, with a small superscript "TM".
3. Two lines of grey-black text: "Soutient naturellement" / "votre système circulatoire*"
4. A solid ORANGE STRIP with white caps text: "COMPLÉMENT ALIMENTAIRE"
5. Large dark-grey caps: "RENFORCE"
6. Large ORANGE-RED caps with a red heart glyph: "LE CŒUR ♥"
7. Dark-grey caps: "ET LES ARTÈRES"
8. Below the curve, the lower third of the label is SOLID ORANGE, plain.

## Brand palette
- Signal orange (#F2701C-ish) — primary, the lower label block and the strip
- Red (#E5352B-ish) — "LE CŒUR" and the heart glyph
- Charcoal / near-black — body copy and "RENFORCE" / "ET LES ARTÈRES"
- White — bottle body, cap, upper label
- Deep navy — used in ad graphics (the ♂ symbol), not on the pack

## Ad-system palette (from the 5 winners, two registers)
- DARK register: near-black background, deep crimson arterial imagery, orange accents
- CLEAN register: white/off-white background, orange and red type, clinical props

## NEVER (exclusion list for every prompt)
No amber or brown glass. No dropper, pipette or rubber bulb. No pump. No sachet or pouch.
No blister pack. No dark or black cap. No gold or silver label. No lotus or floral motif.
The cap is WHITE and RIBBED; the label is WHITE ABOVE, ORANGE BELOW, split by a curve.
