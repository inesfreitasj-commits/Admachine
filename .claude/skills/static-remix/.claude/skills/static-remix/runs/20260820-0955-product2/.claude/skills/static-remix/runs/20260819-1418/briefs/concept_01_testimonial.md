# Concept 01 — TESTIMONIAL (quote-over-photo - their #1 winning format)

## Scene
A candid, warm, editorial photograph inside a French bedroom at night. A woman in her late fifties with tousled blonde hair lies back on white bedlinen, eyes closed, head tilted, an expression of quiet relief and pleasure. Warm bedside lamp light, soft shadows, intimate but entirely non-explicit - shoulders and face only, covered by the sheet. The ÉveilSens bottle stands on the bedside table in the foreground, label facing camera.

## Text overlays
Large bold white text across the lower third, three lines, in quotation marks:
« IL M'A MIS 2 GOUTTES.
10 MINUTES APRÈS, JE LUI AI DIT
DE NE PAS S'ARRÊTER. »

## Headline
« Il m'a mis 2 gouttes. 10 minutes après, je lui ai dit de ne pas s'arrêter. »

## Caption
Plus de 60 000 clientes vérifiées. 1 à 2 gouttes, 90 secondes, et tout change. Garantie satisfait ou remboursé 60 jours.

## Variation axis: camera angle
- var_01: Camera at eye level beside the bed, horizontal framing.
- var_02: Camera directly overhead looking down at the pillow, the woman's head centred, bottle at the frame edge.
